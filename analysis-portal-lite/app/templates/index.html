    What to average
    Average x at each y potentiostatic
    Voltage is the setpoint and current is measured, so current is averaged at each voltage. The usual choice for a polarization curve.
    Average y at each x galvanostatic
    Current is the setpoint and voltage is measured, so voltage is averaged at each current.
